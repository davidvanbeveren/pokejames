# HACKTIVISTS — Violet Version

A Pokémon Red-style browser RPG made as a birthday present for James Morgan, Executive Director of Vegan Hacktivists.
Instead of catching monsters you feed, help and rescue farm animals; instead of fighting, your animals win over
skeptics with charm. Every item is vegan (NOOCH heals HP), VEGAN BEANS are hidden everywhere, and the teams of
Vegan Hacktivists and Violet Studios walk around as NPCs. The world starts in Game Boy black-and-white in Pallet Town,
turns Game Boy Color in Verdant Town, and becomes GBA-quality in Violet City, where a surprise party is waiting.

## Play

- Open `dist/index.html` in any modern browser (keep `dist/audio/` next to it), or serve the folder and open `index.html`.
- Controls: Arrows / WASD move · Z or Space = A · X = B · Enter = START (menu) · Shift = SELECT (bike). Hold B to run in Violet City. M mutes and unmutes all sound.
- On phones a touch D-pad and buttons appear automatically.
- Progress: pick a starter at PROF. OAT's lab, rescue animals in tall grass (FEED / HELP), debate skeptics, heal at a
  SANCTUARY CENTER, wake the sleeping DUCK on Route 2 with OATS, beat CHEF at the OUTREACH GYM, and bring the badge to VIOLET HALL.
- 20 VEGAN BEANS are hidden across the world (10 unlock the BIKE). The legendary PLESIOSAUR perches in Violet City — bring SEEDS.
- 151 creatures to meet, in 76 evolution lines. `creatures/` holds every sprite as a PNG; `creatures/ROSTER.md` lists the lines.

## Project layout

- `index.html` — page, CSS, touch controls, script tags, `<audio>` elements. `python3 build.py` bundles everything into
  `dist/index.html` and copies `audio/` beside it; `--embed` inlines the music as data URIs instead (only viable at low bitrates).
- `js/engine.js` — canvas, three rendering eras, sprites, text, input, maps, entities, camera, script coroutines.
- `js/ui.js` — textbox, menus, party, bag, FriendDex, trainer card, options, name entry, title.
- `js/battle.js` — wild encounters (trust) and skeptic debates (HP/will), evolution, whiteout.
- `js/game.js` — story scripts, shops, centers, PC, save/load, boot.
- `js/data.js` — moves, items, skeptics, encounter tables. `js/npcs.js` — team roster and dialogue.
- `js/creatures_data.js`, `js/creatures_art.js` — the 151 species and their sprites, generated by
  `tools/make_creatures.py` + `tools/export_creatures.py` (edit the roster there, then re-export).
- `js/maps_*.js` — ASCII maps (format in `MAP_BRIEF.md`). `js/sprites_*.js`, `js/font.js` — text pixel art (format in `ART_SPEC.md`).
- `js/audio.js` — Web Audio chiptune engine (original music + all sound effects), plus a streamed-music layer that plays
  the recordings in `audio/` through the same mute/volume/fade controls. Ids in `STREAMS` shadow the synthesized song of
  the same name, which stays as the fallback if a recording is missing. `audio/` holds one mp3 per music id:
  `intro` (title + PROF. OAT intro), `pallet`, `lab`, `route1`, `route` (Route 2), `town2` (Verdant), `town3` (Violet),
  `center` (SANCTUARY CENTERs), `wild` and `trainer` (battles, these restart from the top rather than resuming).
  `victory`, `evolve`, `gym` and `party` are still chiptune.

## Tools

- `node tools/lint.js` — whole-game consistency check (sprites, maps, warps, reachability, scripts, text widths).
- `node tools/playtest.js` — automated full playthrough in a headless engine (title → party).
- `node tools/render.js js/<sprites>.js` and `node tools/rendermap.js js/<maps>.js` — PNG previews in `tools/out/`.
- `node tools/stamp_assets.js` — stamp `?v=<content hash>` on the js/audio URLs in `index.html`. Run before deploying:
  GitHub Pages caches assets for 10 minutes, so without it a fresh deploy keeps serving the old scripts.
- `node tools/export_copy.js` — dump every player-facing line into `COPY.md` with stable `[id]` tags, for editing
  and handing back. Covers story, signs, NPCs, the team's birthday messages, skeptics, items, charms and all 151 dex entries.
- `tools/audiotest.html` — audition every song, recording and sound effect.
- `tools/encode_music.sh <source> <music-id>` — encode a track into `audio/<music-id>.mp3`, loudness-normalised to -18 LUFS
  so every track sits at the same level and they can share one `STREAM_TRIM`. `BITRATE=64k` to shrink for `--embed`.
- In the browser console, `G.tick(n)` and `G.press('a')` drive the game for scripted testing.
